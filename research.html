Core Research Pillars

1. Defensive AI & Adversarial Robustness

Building robust intrusion detection methods to counter Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) and fake command injection attacks across Industrial Control Systems (ICS), Smart Grids, and IoT.

2. Quantum Security & QML

Developing hybrid Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) and Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) protocols for scalable key delivery across 6G, Smart Grids, and distributed sensor networks.

3. Generative AI for Cyber Threat Detection

Deploying generative models (CTGAN, TVAE) to synthesize rare attack samples, addressing extreme class imbalances in Network Intrusion Detection Systems (NIDS).

4. Blockchain & Secure Data Governance

Designing decentralized access-control protocols for privacy-preserving data sharing in Electronic Health Records (EHR) and regional agri-food supply chains.
