Biography

Cyber security academic and researcher with a PhD in Computer Science from Monash University and extensive experience in higher education teaching, research, supervision and academic service. Current research spans cyber security, AI/ML for cyberattack and intrusion detection, adversarial machine learning, IoT and cyber-physical systems security, privacy-preserving technologies, blockchain security, and post-quantum/quantum security. Experienced in coordinating and teaching undergraduate and postgraduate cyber security and networking units, developing innovative learning materials and assessments, leading collaborative research projects, attracting research funding, and working with industry and multidisciplinary partners.

Strategic Vision

  • Defensive AI & Adversarial Robustness: Building systems that resist attacks for Industrial Control Systems (ICS), Smart Grids, and IoT.
  • Quantum Cybersecurity & Key Distribution: Creating mixed quantum and classical encryption methods for strong 6G and distributed networks.
  • HDR & Research Leadership: Recruiting and mentoring high-caliber PhD candidates in quantum machine learning and zero-trust IoT.
  • Research-Informed Pedagogy: Embedding ALTA-funded AI pedagogy and simulation tools into undergraduate and postgraduate coursework.
